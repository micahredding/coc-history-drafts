Week 0 · Introduction & Overview
What this class is

A movement drawn together from reformers, farmers, clergy, and laypeople of many traditions took root on the American frontier — and helped reshape the Christian imagination of millions.

That movement was about ideas — and the practices that embodied those ideas.

We explore that story to discover what is genuinely beautiful, and what is worth carrying forward with vision and imagination.

How each week works
Story→ Idea→ Future

One of us tells a story from our history. Another cross-examines it. Then the questions are yours:

What is beautiful here?
What do we carry forward?

What they called themselves
“Reformers” Campbell's own word — “this current reformation”
“Christians” Stone's side — nothing more, nothing less
“Disciples” Campbell's preference — the New Testament's own word

And what they refused: any founder's name. “Campbellite” was always someone else's word.

Where the movement lives today
Churches of Christ that's us
Christian Churches the independent congregations
Disciples of Christ the organized denomination

One movement — three families. This class is the family story, told from our branch.
