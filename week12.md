# Week 12 — The Story of Governance

[← All weeks](.)

**The Story:** The conviction of the priesthood of all believers, expressed in the Brush Run Ordination, was developed into a deeply anti-authoritarian theology. A plurality of spiritual gifts and roles necessitated a plurality of leadership. Even the legitimacy of a located preacher was debated for a century—and when it was finally accepted, the terms were clear: the minister was _under_ the elders, and was not even allowed to serve the Lord's Supper.

**The Idea**: Our movement is deeply anti-authoritarian, drawing from its conviction of the priesthood of all believers. And the understanding that in the church, God has truly unleashed a _wild democracy_.
